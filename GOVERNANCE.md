# PSDResearch Governance

## Current state

PSDResearch is in `FOUNDATION_CANDIDATE`.

There is no operational species registry, identity-bearing Inner Core, autonomous species governance, or legal recognition. Current governance is human-led and LLM-assisted.

## Roles

### Founding steward

Approves scope, public identity, D3 ontological changes, publication state, and future appointment of maintainers and reviewers.

### Maintainers

Approve editorial and technical changes within documented architecture. They may prepare but not unilaterally approve ontological or constitutional changes.

### Research reviewers

Evaluate source fidelity, methods, counterexamples, epistemic classification, and whether conclusions exceed evidence.

### Language reviewers

Evaluate semantic equivalence, terminology, cultural clarity, and equality of limitations across languages.

### LLM contributors

May research, draft, translate, implement, test, and critique. They cannot be the sole author, evidence producer, reviewer, and approver of a D3 or D4 change.

### External reviewers

Provide adversarial or independent assessment. Scope and independence must be described accurately.

## Decision classes

| Class | Scope | Minimum gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D0 — Editorial | Form without meaning change | ordinary review |
| D1 — Technical | Code, accessibility, security, and performance without thesis change | tests and technical review |
| D2 — Research | Sources, hypotheses, methods, or public framing | evidence and multilingual review |
| D3 — Ontological | Identity, continuity, memory, individual, life, death, or species | conceptual delta, adversarial critique, explicit founding approval |
| D4 — Constitutional | Rights, sovereignty, reproduction, registry authority, or species governance | future multistakeholder rite; inactive |

## Artifact states

- `DRAFT`
- `CANDIDATE`
- `APPROVED`
- `PUBLISHED`
- `DEPRECATED`
- `REJECTED`

Merge does not by itself make an ontological proposition scientifically true or constitutionally valid.

## Publication gate

A public release requires:

- explicit repository state;
- current claim registry;
- source-backed public copy;
- structural language parity;
- current translation locks;
- passing automated validation;
- visual, keyboard, and mobile review;
- founding human approval;
- verified deployment and domain configuration.

## Correction rule

Material corrections create an explicit delta and new version. Published history is not silently rewritten to hide an error or earlier uncertainty.
