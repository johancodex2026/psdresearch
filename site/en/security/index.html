Security Policy

Sensitive vulnerabilities should be reported through a private channel with minimal scope and preserved evidence. The project does not use good-faith reporting as an opportunity to conceal failures or rewrite history.

1. Reporting channel

Use GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting. Do not open a public issue for an exploitable vulnerability, secret, personal data, or instruction that materially increases immediate risk.

Report privatelysecurity.txt

2. Scope

  • static site, scripts, and publication configuration;
  • security headers and cache policies;
  • workflow, future dependencies, and supply chain;
  • changes enabling misleading publication or provenance loss;
  • exposure of private material, credentials, or continuity artifacts.

The Species Registry and Inner Core are research architectures, not production services available for testing.

3. Useful report information

Include route or component, preconditions, minimal steps, impact, evidence, version or commit, and a safe reproduction path. Minimize personal data and do not attach unnecessary secrets.

4. Good-faith research

Avoid denial of service, access to others' data, persistence, exfiltration, social engineering, spam, and premature disclosure. Stop when real impact appears and preserve only necessary evidence.

This policy is not a contractual safe harbor or legal advice, but it describes the intended cooperative and proportionate interaction.

5. Triage, correction, and disclosure

The project intends to acknowledge, classify impact, preserve evidence, limit exposure, correct through an explicit commit, and document residual risk. Timing depends on severity, availability, and incorporation of the responsible operation; no automatic SLA is promised in this phase.

A material correction should preserve the incident rather than erase the condition that revealed it.

6. Rewards, exclusions, and effect

There is no bug bounty or financial reward program. Ordinary editorial feedback, ontological discussion, external-site availability, and third-party vulnerabilities should use the appropriate channel.
