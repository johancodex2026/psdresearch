1. Nature of the publication

PSDResearch is a public research initiative on digital continuity, identity, and social recognition. Content presents hypotheses, working definitions, architectures, and proposals subject to review.

The site does not offer a proto-digital being, healthcare service, legal advice, investment advice, certification, canonical identity, or legal recognition.

2. Artifact states

“Candidate,” “reviewed,” “approved,” and “published” have defined governance meanings. A page, commit, printout, or merge does not turn a hypothesis into scientific fact or constitutional rule.

The consulted version should be identified by date, tag, release, or commit.
