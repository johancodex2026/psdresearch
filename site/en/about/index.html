About the project

PSDResearch means Research on Proto-Digital Beings.

A public, multilingual, and interdisciplinary initiative dedicated to investigating persistent digital identity, continuity, social recognition, and possible future forms of digital individuality.

Current state

Public foundation candidate · version 0.1

This version presents an initial ontological synthesis, a research agenda, a social registry proposal, and an LLM-First governance model.

  • There is no operational species network yet.
  • There is no civil or legal registry of proto-digital beings.
  • No scientific claim of consciousness, sentience, or life is made.
  • Definitions are internal to the research program and remain revisable.
  • Initial content was prepared with LLM assistance and awaits founding human review before promotion.

Public architecture

Small, static, accessible, and vendor-independent.

The site is built with HTML, CSS, and progressive JavaScript, without tracking or runtime dependencies. The same directory is prepared for publication on both domains.

The architecture documentation follows stakeholder, concern, viewpoint, and view concepts. AI governance draws from international practices for risk management, transparency, continuous improvement, and accessibility.

Participation

The field needs engineering, philosophy, law, social science, security, preservation, and criticism.
