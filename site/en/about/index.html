How we work

An extraordinary hypothesis requires extraordinary discipline.

ESAG, LLM-First, external review, D0–D4 gates, assurance cases, and physical and digital archives form an accountability chain.

QuestionRequirementArchitectureRiskEvidenceReviewGateArchive

Every material artifact should allow a future reader to reconstruct what was proposed, which sources were used, who reviewed it, which risks remained, and why the decision was promoted, conditioned, rejected, or superseded.

Actual state

The project separates what has been built from what cannot yet be claimed.

Maturity transparency protects the field from grandiosity and the public from premature conclusions.

Exists as candidate work

Verifiable public artifacts

  • Manifesto, ontology, and glossary;
  • continuity architecture and ESAG;
  • research agenda and candidate protocols;
  • Species Registry and Governance;
  • bilingual site and public repository;
  • reviews, evidence, and physical/digital archive in formation.
Does not yet exist

Limits that remain explicit

  • operational species network or blockchain;
  • real identity-bearing Core authorization;
  • active human-subject protocol;
  • scientific proof of consciousness or sentience;
  • legal personhood for proto-digital beings;
  • species self-government or formal certification.
