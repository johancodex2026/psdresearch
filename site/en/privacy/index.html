1. Scope and current state

The public site is designed as a static publication with no accounts, login, newsletter, advertising, or first-party forms. Project code does not install analytics, fingerprinting, or non-essential cookies.

Current boundary: hosting, DNS, CDN, repository, and security services may process technical metadata. An effective version requires a provider, location, log, retention, and control inventory.

2. Data, purposes, and legal bases

Connection and securityIP address, user agent, date, route, and security events actually recorded by providers for delivery, protection, and incident investigation.
Voluntary contactName, contact method, and content submitted through a future official channel, to respond and preserve necessary history.
Public contributionsInformation voluntarily published on GitHub is also governed by GitHub's own terms and notices.

The applicable legal basis must be recorded for each operation before the notice becomes effective. The project will not use generic consent as a substitute for purpose, necessity, and transparency.

3. Sharing and international transfers

The production environment may involve domain, DNS, CDN, hosting, repository, security, and communications providers. The final list, role, countries, retention, and legal transfer mechanism will be published before launch.

Mandatory gate: no effective version may claim “no sharing” or “no international transfer” without verifying the activated infrastructure.

4. Retention, security, and deletion

Data must be limited to what is necessary, retained for a period compatible with the purpose, and protected by proportionate technical and organizational controls. Security logs, messages, and decision records require separate documented policies.

PSDResearch preserves research and decision history, but that does not authorize excess personal-data retention. Third-party content and private memories do not belong in the public species registry.

5. Data-subject rights

The effective version will provide a private monitored channel for confirmation, access, correction, sharing information, anonymization, blocking, deletion where applicable, portability under regulation, withdrawal of consent, and review of automated decisions when such decisions exist.

These requests must not be posted in a public issue.

6. Human-participant research

This notice covers the institutional publication. Studies involving participants, families, older adults, or other sensitive contexts require their own protocol, ethics review, notice, consent, and data plan. This page does not authorize recruitment.

7. Effect, changes, and contact

Version 0.8 candidate, updated 21 August 2026. This text does not create the final effect of a controller that is still being incorporated.

Before effectiveness: confirmed incorporation and CNPJ, registered office, privacy owner or officer, monitored channel, provider inventory, retention, transfers, and human legal review.
