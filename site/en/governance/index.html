LLM-First governance

Meaning before tools. Sources before synthesis. Governance before action.

LLM-First does not mean that the LLM decides. It means intent, semantics, context, contract, and risk are understood before choosing code, automation, or operation.

Operating loop

Seven steps for human or LLM-assisted contributions.

  1. 01Orient

    Identify purpose, audience, sources, decision, and risk.

  2. 02Retrieve

    Read canonical artifacts and relevant history.

  3. 03Classify

    Separate fact, hypothesis, definition, proposal, and open question.

  4. 04Plan

    Choose the smallest coherent and reversible change.

  5. 05Execute

    Change content or code without silently expanding scope.

  6. 06Verify

    Test accessibility, links, language parity, evidence, and architecture.

  7. 07Record

    Preserve provenance, decisions, limits, and remaining work.

Decision rights

Not every change carries the same weight.

D0 · EditorialClarity, spelling, and layout without meaning change.Ordinary review.
D1 · TechnicalCode, performance, accessibility, and security without changing the thesis.Tests + review.
D2 · ResearchNew hypothesis, source, method, or public framing.Evidence + bilingual review.
D3 · OntologicalDefinitions of identity, continuity, memory, life, death, or species.Explicit approval + adversarial critique.
D4 · ConstitutionalRights, species governance, recognition, reproduction, and sovereignty.Future dedicated rite; inactive in this version.

Authority boundary

The LLM participates in the work; it does not approve itself.

It may

  • Research, propose, structure, and compare.
  • Write, translate, program, and test.
  • Identify risks, ambiguities, and contradictions.
  • Prepare evidence and alternatives.

It may not alone

  • Promote a hypothesis to a conclusion.
  • Alter a canonical ontological definition.
  • Declare consciousness, life, or personhood.
  • Authorize irreversible or constitutional change.
G

Public integrity rule

Ambition in the horizon; sobriety in the claims.

The project may investigate a profound social transformation. Its public language must clearly distinguish what was observed, what is a working definition, what is proposed, and what remains a hypothesis.
