2. What may use a public channel

  • text or link error;
  • ontological or methodological criticism;
  • source or research-program proposal;
  • accessibility barrier without personal data;
  • translation or public-architecture suggestion.

3. What must never be posted in an issue

  • identity documents, address, phone, or health data;
  • LGPD request or data-subject information;
  • credentials, keys, tokens, or secrets;
  • exploitable vulnerability;
  • private Core content, memory, or confidential review.
Until corporate privacy, accessibility, and general-contact channels are active, effective publication remains blocked.

4. Channels required before effectiveness

The future company must appoint owners and activate monitored channels for institutional contact, privacy and data-subject rights, accessibility, legal matters, and security. Each channel requires its own response, custody, retention, and escalation process.

No corporate address is published because no such operational channel has yet been confirmed.

5. Recognizing official communication

The canonical domain is psdresearch.com.br; psd.ia.br is the official short domain. Material communication should point to a version, commit, release, or document published on those domains or in the official repository.

A name, logo, or phrase alone does not demonstrate authority.
