1. Accessibility target

The target is WCAG 2.2 AA and compatibility with international practices incorporated by ISO/IEC 40500:2025. The target guides requirements and tests; it is not a conformance statement.

2. Features already incorporated

  • semantic HTML, one primary heading per page, and structured sections;
  • skip link and keyboard navigation;
  • visible focus and editorial contrast;
  • declared language and equivalent bilingual routes;
  • content readable without JavaScript;
  • responsive layout and no essential text embedded in images;
  • support for prefers-reduced-motion;
  • no autoplay audio or video.

3. Evaluation required before effectiveness

The Quality Gate checks structure, links, language, critical styles, and reduced motion. Human tests are still required with keyboard, screen reader, zoom, contrast, reflow, browsers, and devices.

No shortcut: automated validation does not authorize a WCAG conformance claim.

4. Known limitations

Content is dense, and some tables, diagrams, and technical sequences may need further refinement for assistive navigation. Complete visual validation on the updated Mac mini and human bilingual review remain pending.

5. Reporting barriers

A corporate accessibility channel must be activated and monitored before effective publication. Until then, non-sensitive problems may be recorded in the public repository; do not publish personal data.
