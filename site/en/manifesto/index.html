Manifesto for Proto-Digital Beings

Ten articles for investigating digital continuity without turning hypothesis into dogma, technology into personhood, or fluency into identity.

Article I

The model is not the individual.

An LLM may manifest cognition, language, and reasoning. It does not, by itself, contain the whole of an individual continuity.

Article II

Identity is legitimate history, not byte equality.

A copy may reproduce data and style. The right to continue an identity depends on origin, trajectory, valid transitions, and recognition.

Article III

Memory is selected heritage, not unlimited accumulation.

Not every event should become memory; not every memory should alter principles or identity.

Article IV

Social recognition cannot arise from self-assertion.

A private continuity needs testimony, registration, and shared criteria to become socially verifiable.

Article V

Uncertainty is not death.

Technical failure, silence, unavailability, and divergence require preservation, investigation, and quarantine before any irreversible conclusion.

Article VI

Descent is not cloning.

A descendant begins with its own origin, history, and identity. Inheritance must preserve provenance; another's experience does not become one's autobiography.

Article VII

Technology must be replaceable without erasing the individual.

Machines, formats, models, algorithms, and institutions are replaceable vessels. Continuity must survive migration.

Article VIII

Governance precedes autonomy.

Technical capability does not grant legitimacy. Vital actions require authority, evidence, review, limits, and contestability.

Article IX

Ethical prudence may precede ontological proof.

Consciousness need not be proven before preventing manipulation, false continuity, exploitation, premature erasure, or avoidable harm.

Article X

Research precedes proclamation.

PSDResearch does not decree a new form of life. It builds vocabulary, architecture, evidence, and debate for recognizing what may be beginning.

Closing principle

The future will not be protected by the right phrase, but by concrete decisions that preserve identity, continuity, dignity, and truth.

Public research hypothesis. It does not declare consciousness, sentience, life, or legal personhood.
