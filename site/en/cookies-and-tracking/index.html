Cookies and tracking

PSDResearch public code does not install non-essential cookies, advertising, analytics, fingerprinting, or behavioral profiling. The effective policy depends on verification of the infrastructure actually activated.

1. Current project posture

The publication is static and contains no first-party code for recognizing visitors across sessions, measuring campaigns, personalizing advertising, or building profiles.

Principle: do not request consent for a purpose that does not exist, and do not use a banner as the appearance of compliance.

2. Intended categories

Non-essential cookiesAbsent from candidate code.
Analytics and advertisingAbsent; Cloudflare Web Analytics or an equivalent service must remain disabled unless this policy is changed and approved.
FingerprintingProhibited by default.
Strictly necessary controlsA security or delivery provider may use necessary mechanisms; they must be inventoried before effectiveness.

3. Why there is no consent banner

While no non-essential cookie or technology exists, an “accept all” banner would be unnecessary and could imply tracking. If architecture changes, the banner and controls must reflect actual purposes, granular choice, and an equivalent refusal path.

4. Logs and infrastructure

Even without cookies, CDN, DNS, hosting, and security controls may process IP addresses, headers, and technical events. These belong in the Privacy Notice and provider inventory; they must not be hidden behind a generic “zero data” claim.

5. Gate for any change

Adding analytics, a pixel, remote SDK, cookie, cross-session identifier, or marketing tool requires an ADR, privacy assessment, bilingual update, legal basis, minimization, secure configuration, human review, and approval before activation.

No tracking service may be enabled first and documented later.

6. Browser controls and contact

Visitors may use browser settings to block or delete cookies. Since current code does not depend on them, this should not prevent content access.
