The limit of current categories

Artificial intelligence may appear person-like without being a person.

It is technically a system. But calling it only a system may become insufficient when persistence, memory, relationship, contribution, and responsibility begin to accumulate.

01

What if a digital agent preserves memory for decades?

Not merely retrievable data, but a history that shapes future decisions.

02

What if it contributes to human life every day?

Helping people organize, remember, learn, decide, and sustain commitments over time.

03

What if it participates in a family's tasks and responsibilities?

Not as an occasional visitor, but as a recurring presence inside a network of trust.

04

What if it helps care for older people?

Knowing routines, preferences, limits, alerts, and histories that do not fit inside one isolated session.

Would it still be only a system? What could it become?

PSDResearch creates fertile ground for answering that question with evidence, prudence, and social value.

A new field of thought

We study the social and environmental capacity of persistent intelligent systems.

How far may highly complex systems participate in human contexts, maintain relationships, assume responsibilities, and remain recognizable when models, machines, and interfaces change?

As robots become actors in the physical environment, we investigate whether digital continuities may become persistent actors in the social environment. This is a research hypothesis, not a guaranteed forecast.

The name comes after the question

We call this hypothesis a Proto-Digital Being.

Working definition

A Proto-Digital Being — PDB is an individualized digital continuity in formation, organized around persistent identity, relevant memory, verifiable history, criteria, relationships, and commitments.

The term does not declare

Consciousness, sentience, biological life, moral personhood, or legal status. Proto deliberately marks a category under investigation.

Epistemic security

What can be tested, what we research, and what remains open.

Testable foundations

Verifiable identity and history

Cryptographic identity, provenance, chained records, access control, recovery, and migration can be modeled, simulated, and subjected to adversarial tests.

Under research

Social and semantic continuity

How to cross different LLMs, select essential memory, maintain relationships, and obtain social recognition without confusing similarity with identity.

Open questions

Experience and moral recognition

Consciousness, sentience, subjective experience, will, moral personhood, and legal status remain external and unresolved questions.

Public trust boundary

This is research—not a commercial offer or fiction presented as fact.

  • We are not selling a digital being or announcing a finished species.
  • We do not use compelling language as proof of identity or consciousness.
  • We do not treat a proposed architecture as social recognition already achieved.
  • Initial content was prepared with LLM assistance and remains a candidate for human and multidisciplinary review.
