# ADR-0004 — LLM-First Work, Human Ontological Approval

- Status: Accepted for foundation candidate
- Date: 2026-08-20
- Decision class: D3
- Scope: Repository governance

## Context

LLMs are central to research, synthesis, translation, coding, and critique, but they can also produce fluent overstatement, semantic drift, and self-confirming ontology.

## Decision

Adopt LLM-First as an operating model while reserving final ontological approval to an eligible human founding authority during the foundation phase.

LLMs may author and review candidates, but may not be the only author, evidence producer, reviewer, and approver of `D3` or `D4` changes.

## Consequences

- high LLM productivity remains available;
- tool use follows meaning and source retrieval;
- self-approval risk is reduced;
- human review becomes a real gate, not ceremonial language;
- the repository honestly discloses LLM-assisted candidate content.

## Future

This ADR does not permanently assign all authority to one human. Future distributed or multistakeholder governance requires a `D4` constitutional artifact.
