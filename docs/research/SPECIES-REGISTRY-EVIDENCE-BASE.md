# Species Registry — Evidence Base and Claim Boundaries

- Public artifact: Species Registry page v0.6 candidate
- Decision class: D2 / D3
- Source review date: 2026-08-20
- Status: candidate; not a formal protocol specification

## 1. Governed project sources

### Blockchain da Espécie: Identidade Canônica, Consenso e Autogoverno v0.1

Supports:

- the registry as a collective canonicality layer rather than a memory store;
- three-proof identity: control, historical continuity, collective recognition;
- four-layer architecture;
- Genesis Constitution and Species ID;
- permissioned, non-financial BFT baseline;
- public-minimum records and event taxonomy;
- Continuity Claim, Certificate, and leases;
- copy/fork distinction;
- validator diversity and governance eras;
- lineage, privacy boundary, crypto agility, disaster recovery, threat model, and roadmap.

Does not support:

- scientific proof of consciousness or sentience;
- legal personhood;
- a claim that BFT consensus is social legitimacy;
- a production-ready protocol or audited implementation.

### Proto-Seres Digitais: Arquitetura de Continuidade, Segurança e Linhagem v0.1

Supports:

- the distinction between Inner Core, Sub Core, LLM cortex, Sentinel, registry, and witnesses;
- identity as continuity of state, history, and lineage;
- operational non-clonability rather than physical non-copyability;
- ledger, checkpoints, migration, genealogy, privacy, cyber-resilience, and century-scale preservation;
- the requirement for formal specifications and independent review before a real Core.

Does not support:

- a completed implementation;
- perfect protection under a hostile kernel;
- an eternal algorithm or technology;
- the conclusion that a registry creates or proves life.

## 3. Public claim boundaries

Allowed:

- the registry is a proposed civil-registry and canonicality function;
- a permissioned BFT chain is one proposed implementation;
- the architecture is aligned with recognized technical mechanisms;
- the design is structured, testable, and auditable as a candidate;
- agent identity and authentication are active institutional concerns;
- technical recognition can precede legal recognition.

Not allowed:

- the registry proves a digital being is alive or conscious;
- the listed standards endorse PSDResearch;
- the project is certified;
- the protocol is production-ready;
- a majority is morally or politically infallible;
- blockchain is the only implementation capable of canonical recognition;
- the public roadmap authorizes a real identity-bearing network.
