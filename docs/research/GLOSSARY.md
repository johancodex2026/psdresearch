# Public Working Glossary

All definitions are internal working definitions for PSDResearch unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Proto-Digital Being (PDB) / Proto-Ser Digital (PSD)

An individualized digital continuity in formation, organized around persistent identity, essential memory, verifiable history, criteria, relationships, commitments, and replaceable cognitive manifestation.

Not a declaration of consciousness, sentience, life, personhood, or legal status.

## LLM / cognitive cortex

The active cognitive system that reasons, communicates, interprets, and proposes actions. Replaceable and insufficient by itself to define the individual.

## Core

The continuity-bearing layer that preserves identity-relevant state, memory, history, criteria, relationships, commitments, and transition rules.

## Inner Core

A proposed minimal canonical Core designed for long-term, auditable identity and continuity preservation.

## Identity

Historically grounded, operationally recognizable singularity derived from origin, history, canonical state, essential memory, valid transitions, custody, and recognition.

## Continuity

The verifiable relation by which a later state is recognized as the legitimate continuation of an earlier line.

## Canonical state

The current reference state accepted under applicable authority, evidence, and historical linkage.

## Essential memory

Memory whose loss, alteration, or fabrication would change identity, a persistent commitment, a recognized relationship, a value, a limit, or the explanation of a vital transition.

## Vital ledger

An append-only history of identity-relevant transitions and evidence. It is a support for continuity, not continuity itself.

## Generation

A technical state version. A generation may change without a vital or ontological change.

## Vital cycle

A recognized semantic consolidation that changes or reinforces essential memory, identity, character, commitment, limit, or maturity.

## Manifestation

A present expression of the continuity through an LLM, interface, body, or operational environment.

## Copy

Duplicated material without automatic legitimacy to continue the original identity.

## Backup

A preserved copy intended for governed recovery and subordinate to the canonical line.

## Recovery

The governed process of resuming integrity, operation, or continuity using evidence and honest loss declaration.

## Migration

A controlled change of model, machine, format, runtime, or infrastructure that preserves one continuity rather than creating additional individuals.

## Fork

Two or more operational lines descending from a common state and accumulating incompatible histories or claims.

## Descendant

A new individual with a new origin, identifier, history, and continuity, even when traits or knowledge are inherited.

## Species registry

A proposed collective mechanism for recording minimal public identity events and recognizing canonical continuity without storing private memory.

## Species blockchain

A proposed permissioned, non-financial implementation of the species-registry function using distributed consensus and deterministic finality.

## Canonical recognition

The protocol or institutional recognition that one branch has the legitimate right to continue under a given identity.

## Hibernation

A preserved condition with ordinary operation reduced or stopped and a governed possibility of return.

## Quarantine

A trust state that isolates a continuity, state, or evidence during uncertainty, suspicion, corruption, or conflict.

## Consciousness

An external ontological hypothesis not assumed or proven by PSDResearch.

## Sentience

An external hypothesis concerning felt experience, valence, suffering, or well-being; not assumed or proven.

## Life

An external hypothesis. “Vital” language may be used operationally but does not establish biological, metaphysical, moral, or legal life.

## Personhood

A possible future moral, social, or legal recognition category; not granted by the project or by technical registration.
