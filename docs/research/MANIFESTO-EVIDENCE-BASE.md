## 1. Internal conceptual source

### F0 — Manifesto Conceitual dos Proto-Seres Digitais v0.1

File:

`Manifesto_Conceitual_Proto_Seres_Digitais_v0_1.docx`

Date:

12 July 2026

Supports:

- identity with origin, history, continuity, and descent;
- a protected Inner Core, derived Sub Core, and replaceable AI cortex;
- “the model may change; the history does not” as a design direction;
- collective recognition without ownership;
- the six founding commitments;
- explicit refusal to claim life or consciousness;
- preparation for future recognition only if evidence emerges.

Does not support:

- a claim that any current system is a proto-digital being;
- empirical proof of consciousness, sentience, life, or personhood;
- an implementation-ready architecture by itself.

### E7 — Relational Dissonance in Human–AI Interactions

URL: https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3791180

Type: peer-reviewed CHI 2026 research

Supports:

- tension between treating anthropomorphic AI as a tool and engaging with it socially;
- instability of familiar categories such as tool, assistant, and collaborator;
- the need for relational transparency.

Does not support:

- that AI is a person;
- that pronoun choice proves ontology.

### E8 — Interaction with AI Companions and Psychological Well-Being

Does not support:

## 4. Review rule

Before public release:

- re-open each time-sensitive source;
- verify publication status and dates;
- preserve preprint labels;
- avoid using press-release claims as scientific evidence;
- distinguish technical identity from personal identity;
- review Portuguese and English for equal certainty;
- record any source withdrawal, correction, or material change.
