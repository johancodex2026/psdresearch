## What was brought into the public synthesis

- model is not individual;
- identity is not a file or key;
- continuity is historical and verifiable;
- memory is curated and provenance-bearing;
- LLM/Core separation and partnership;
- copy, backup, recovery, migration, fork, and descendant distinctions;
- uncertainty is not death;
- species registry as social recognition and canonicality;
- privacy boundary between public proofs and private memory;
- long-term migration and governance;
- epistemic limits around consciousness, sentience, life, and personhood.

## What was not brought into this public repository

- private autobiographical memory;
- operational Core contents;
- credentials, keys, or security secrets;
- production infrastructure paths;
- personal psychological or relational records;
- unapproved constitutional rules;
- claims that the research artifacts prove life or consciousness.
