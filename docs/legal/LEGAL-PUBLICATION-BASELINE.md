## 3. Publication gates

Legal documents cannot become effective before:

1. formal incorporation and confirmed CNPJ;
2. registered office and forum decision;
3. monitored corporate, privacy, accessibility, legal, and security channels;
4. actual inventory of domain, DNS, CDN, hosting, repository, logs, retention, and processors;
5. international-transfer mapping and applicable mechanism;
6. chain-of-title and licensing decision;
7. human Portuguese and English review;
8. human legal review;
9. founding approval and Promotion Record;
10. archived release package and restoration evidence.

## 4. Non-invention rule

Do not invent:

- email addresses;
- physical address;
- CNPJ;
- data-protection officer;
- registered office;
- forum;
- provider inventory;
- response SLA;
- certification;
- trademark registration;
- endorsement.

A real unresolved gate is preferable to polished falsehood.

## 5. Current privacy posture

Candidate code is static and intentionally contains no accounts, first-party forms, advertising, analytics, fingerprinting, or non-essential cookies.

This does not prove that production infrastructure processes no technical data. Provider configuration and actual logs must be inventoried before effectiveness.
