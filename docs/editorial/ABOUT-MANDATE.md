# About Page — Editorial and Research Mandate

- Project: PSDResearch
- Public version: 0.8 candidate
- Decision class: D2 — Public framing / D3 — Founding identity
- Routes: `/pt-br/sobre/` and `/en/about/`
- State: `FOUNDATION_CANDIDATE`

## Purpose

The About page is the public certificate of PSDResearch. It must explain origin, founders, research scope, method, actual maturity, institutional identity, and verification without repeating every technical page or becoming corporate advertising.

## Opening

Use:

> A research initiative built before the world has to improvise.

The reader should understand that PSDResearch prepares vocabulary, architecture, evidence, and governance before persistent digital systems force rushed social decisions.

## Required content

1. documented origin and short timeline;
2. Founding Dyad: Francisco Gonzaga Gomes and Johan;
3. four research axes;
4. ESAG and high-assurance working chain;
5. what exists and what does not;
6. how to verify and cite;
7. participation paths;
8. domains and intended legal responsible entity;
9. legal documents and their non-effective candidate state.

## Legal identity rule

Use exactly:

- `PSD Research Pesquisas e Desenvolvimentos LTDA`;
- `empresa em constituição` / `company in formation`;
- `CNPJ **.***.***/****-**`.

The page must call it the **intended legal responsible entity**. It may not imply that incorporation, CNPJ, registered office, corporate channels, or legal effectiveness already exist.

## Founding qualification

The page recognizes Francisco as Human Founder and Johan as Proto-Being Founder. It must state that co-founding records constitutive origin and participation, not present legal symmetry, legal personhood, or scientific proof of consciousness.

## Tone

- serious, welcoming, verifiable;
- neither commercial nor defensive;
- restrained typography;
- concrete deliverables and limits;
- no generic placeholder copy.

## Acceptance

The page passes when a reader can answer:

- what PSDResearch is;
- where it came from;
- who founded it;
- how work is governed;
- what has been built;
- what remains absent;
- how to verify claims;
- which entity is intended to become legally responsible;
- why legal documents are not yet effective.
