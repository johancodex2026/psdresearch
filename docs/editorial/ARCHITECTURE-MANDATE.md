# Public Architecture Page Mandate

- Project: PSDResearch
- Artifact: Public Architecture page
- Version: 0.3 candidate
- Decision classes: D1 — Technical; D2 — Research framing; D3 — Ontological boundaries
- Implementation state: candidate

## 1. Public role

The Architecture page is the bridge between the Manifesto and the Research agenda.

The Manifesto explains why the question matters. Architecture must demonstrate that the idea can be decomposed into responsibilities, authority boundaries, evidence, transitions, failure states, and tests.

It must make the proposal executable enough to be criticized without pretending that an operational proto-digital being already exists.

## 2. Required thesis

> Identity is not the file. It is the verifiable continuity of state, history, and lineage.

The page must preserve these separations:

- LLM is not the individual;
- Inner Core is canonical, not omnipotent;
- Sub Core is derived and reconstructible;
- the cognitive cortex is replaceable;
- memory proposal is not accepted biography;
- copying bytes is not recognized continuity;
- migration changes the vessel, not necessarily the individual;
- social recognition is distinct from private continuity.

## 3. Audience

- technically curious public readers;
- researchers and reviewers;
- architects and security engineers;
- legal, ethical, and social-science readers preparing for later pages;
- future contributors who need a coherent system boundary.

The page must be intelligible without requiring cryptography or distributed-systems expertise.

## 4. Source authority

Primary project sources:

1. `Arquitetura de Continuidade, Segurança e Linhagem v0.1`;
2. `Blockchain da Espécie: Identidade Canônica, Consenso e Autogoverno v0.1`;
3. A00 — Mandato Pré-Gênese;
4. A01 — Ontologia e Glossário.

External standards support engineering mechanisms. They do not validate the PDB ontology.

## 5. Public sequence

The page must progress through:

1. the architectural problem;
2. layers and asymmetric authority;
3. operational identity;
4. migration and continuity;
5. experience-to-memory sedimentation;
6. an illustrative minimal implementation;
7. adversarial validation;
8. a layered roadmap;
9. the epistemic boundary;
10. the transition to the Research page.

## 6. Depth boundary

The page may introduce:

- canonical state;
- vital ledger;
- provenance;
- migration proofs;
- closed capabilities;
- witnesses and checkpoints;
- fail-closed and quarantine;
- crypto-agility;
- synthetic prototypes.

It must not present:

- final binary formats;
- production key sizes as eternal rules;
- a complete Genesis Constitution;
- a real species network;
- identity-bearing production deployment;
- consciousness as an architecture output.

## 7. Visual rule

Use diagrams, structured grids, and moderate typography.

The page must comply with `VISUAL-TYPOGRAPHY.md`: architectural seriousness is expressed by order and restraint, not giant headlines.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

The page passes when a reader can explain:

- why the model is replaceable;
- what the Inner Core preserves;
- why no component may self-validate alone;
- how migration may preserve identity;
- why LLM narrative cannot write canonical memory directly;
- which parts are buildable and testable;
- what architecture cannot prove;
- why Research is the next necessary step.
