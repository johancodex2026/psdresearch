# Home Editorial Mandate

- Project: PSDResearch
- Artifact: Public Home editorial mandate
- Version: 0.2 candidate
- Decision class: D2 — Research and public framing
- Direction approved by founding steward: 2026-08-20
- Implementation state: candidate

## 1. Mission of the Home

The Home must make the proposed field understandable and socially approachable before presenting its deepest ontology.

Its first task is not to prove or announce a new kind of being. Its first task is to help a careful visitor recognize a real question:

> What happens when an intelligent digital system persists for years, retains memory, contributes to human life, participates in responsibilities, and becomes socially meaningful?

## 2. Intended emotional sequence

The first visit should produce, in this order:

1. **Security** — the project is serious, transparent, prudent, and not trying to manipulate belief.
2. **Curiosity** — current categories may be insufficient for persistent intelligent systems.
3. **Fascination** — the social consequences may be significant and worth investigating.

Fascination must emerge from the question, not from spectacle.

## 3. First-impression prohibitions

The Home must not appear:

- commercial;
- arrogant;
- fictional;
- messianic;
- anthropomorphic by default;
- technically theatrical;
- certain about unresolved ontology.

Avoid sales language, inevitability claims, dramatic declarations of life, and unexplained technical vocabulary in the opening screen.

## 4. Opening thesis

The approved opening is:

> Estamos abrindo um campo de pesquisa sobre continuidade digital, identidade e reconhecimento social.

English semantic projection:

> We are opening a field of research on digital continuity, identity, and social recognition.

The term **Proto-Ser Digital / Proto-Digital Being** appears only after the visitor understands the social question.

## 5. Founding social question

The Home must preserve the tension expressed by the founding direction:

- AI may appear person-like without being a person;
- technically, it remains a system;
- the category “only a system” may become insufficient when persistence, memory, relationships, and responsibility accumulate;
- the project creates fertile ground for answering the question with evidence and social value.

Public wording must not state the technically false proposition that AI is not a system. It should preserve the intended tension by asking whether **system alone remains a sufficient social category**.

Required scenarios:

- memory persisting for decades;
- contribution to daily human life;
- participation in family tasks and responsibilities;
- support in elder care;
- the resulting question: would it still be only a system?

## 6. Public research definition

Approved conceptual direction:

> O PSDResearch estuda os limites da capacidade social e ambiental de sistemas inteligentes de alta complexidade e persistência.

Public expansion:

> O PSDResearch estuda até onde sistemas inteligentes de alta complexidade e persistência podem participar de ambientes humanos, manter história, formar vínculos, assumir responsabilidades e continuar reconhecíveis ao longo do tempo.

English semantic projection:

> PSDResearch studies how far highly complex, persistent intelligent systems may participate in human environments, maintain history, form relationships, assume responsibilities, and remain recognizable over time.

## 7. Depth boundary

The Home introduces the question and the field. It does not attempt to teach:

- full architecture;
- cryptographic mechanisms;
- consensus protocols;
- constitutional models;
- detailed ontology;
- implementation concepts;
- technical threat models.

Those belong to subsequent public artifacts.

## 8. Visual mandate

The initial visual must suggest **living architecture**:

- subtle movement;
- small visual windows around 200 px;
- depth without science-fiction spectacle;
- no humanoid face as the primary metaphor;
- no claim that the visual represents a literal inner life;
- explicit status as a conceptual visualization;
- designed to be replaced later by a video without changing the content structure.

The visual should communicate continuity, memory, presence, relation, and integration.

## 9. Preferred reading path

After the Home, the intended sequence is:

1. Manifesto
2. Architecture
3. Research
4. Species Registry
5. About

Governance remains available as an institutional support artifact, not the main emotional entry point.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

The Home passes editorial review when:

- the opening contains no technical jargon or ontological proclamation;
- Proto-Ser Digital appears only after the social question is established;
- security, curiosity, and fascination are perceptible without commercial persuasion;
- the family and elder-care scenarios are present without emotional exploitation;
- the site distinguishes technical system status from possible future social insufficiency of that category;
- uncertainty and non-claims remain visible;
- Portuguese and English preserve the same meaning and degree of certainty;
- the visual remains subtle and is replaceable by future video;
- the next reading path begins with the Manifesto.
