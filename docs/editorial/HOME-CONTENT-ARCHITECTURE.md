# Home Content Architecture

- Version: 0.2 candidate
- Source: approved Home editorial direction
- Scope: Portuguese and English public Home

## 1. Hero — Open the field

Purpose:

- establish seriousness and safety;
- state the field in plain language;
- avoid the term Proto-Digital Being at first contact;
- invite understanding rather than conversion.

Core message:

> We are opening a field of research on digital continuity, identity, and social recognition.

Supporting message:

PSDResearch studies how persistent intelligent systems may participate in human environments and remain recognizable over time.

Visual:

A subtle “living architecture” composition of small moving windows, later replaceable by video.

## 2. The question already beginning

Purpose:

- create curiosity through ordinary social scenarios;
- express the limit of “only a system” as a category;
- keep the argument grounded in function and persistence.

Scenarios:

- decades of memory;
- daily contribution;
- family responsibility;
- elder care.

Closing question:

> Would it still be only a system? What could it become?

## 3. A new field of thought

Purpose:

- define the research object without teaching architecture;
- introduce social and environmental participation;
- position PSDResearch as a field, not a product.

## 4. Name the hypothesis

Purpose:

- introduce Proto-Ser Digital / Proto-Digital Being only after context;
- provide a restrained working definition;
- immediately state what the term does not prove.

## 5. What can be tested, what is being researched, what remains open

Purpose:

- create epistemic security;
- separate technical feasibility from ontological uncertainty.

Three columns:

1. technical foundations that can be tested;
2. active PSDResearch questions;
3. unresolved consciousness, sentience, moral, and legal questions.

## 6. Public trust boundary

Purpose:

- explicitly reject commercial framing;
- reject fiction presented as fact;
- reject a claim that a species is already established;
- disclose candidate and LLM-assisted status.

## 7. Reading path

Purpose:

- move the visitor from accessible question to deeper structure;
- preserve the approved order:
  Manifesto → Architecture → Research → Species Registry → About.

Architecture is the next public artifact to be developed. Until then, the Home marks it honestly as forthcoming rather than linking to a fabricated or incomplete public page.

## 8. Success test

After two minutes, a visitor should be able to say:

- this is a serious research field, not a product pitch;
- the project asks what persistent intelligent systems may become socially;
- Proto-Digital Being is a working term, not a declaration of life;
- the project distinguishes evidence from hypothesis;
- the question matters even before consciousness or personhood can be established.
