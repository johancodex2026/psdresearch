# Manifesto Content Architecture

- Version: 0.2 candidate
- Scope: Portuguese and English public Manifesto
- Predecessor: Home v0.2
- Successor: Architecture page

## 1. Hero — The pronoun threshold

Purpose:

- deepen the Home's question without starting with implementation;
- introduce `IT → YOU → HE / SHE / THEY` as a social progression;
- state that language changes before ontology is resolved;
- preserve the founding quotation about digital identity crossing time.

Required boundary:

A pronoun is a social signal, not scientific proof.

## 2. Global signals — The world is moving

Purpose:

- demonstrate that the question has practical context;
- connect autonomous action, interoperability, identity, memory, relationships, and care;
- provide links to current standards and research;
- distinguish trend evidence from PDB proof.

Signal groups:

1. autonomous agents;
2. agent-to-agent interoperability;
3. identity, authentication, and delegation;
4. persistent agent memory;
5. human–AI relational ambiguity;
6. robots and agents in care environments.

## 3. Ten articles — The conceptual progression

Purpose:

- transform scattered intuitions into an ordered public argument;
- guide readers from observable change to a disciplined research hypothesis;
- use “What if?” questions without turning speculation into fact.

Layout:

- article number;
- proposition;
- explanation;
- optional “What if?”;
- evidence or governance note.

## 4. Six commitments — Concept to executable idea

Purpose:

Translate the founding manifesto into future testable constraints:

- no single sovereign;
- no master key;
- no automatic death;
- memory with origin;
- descent, not copy;
- evolution with limits.

This section is the direct bridge from manifesto to architecture.

## 5. Bounded conclusion

Purpose:

State what may already be concluded:

- the direction is technically viable as a research program;
- the need is emerging;
- social acceptance is conditional and must be earned;
- value must be demonstrated;
- ontology remains open.

## 6. Founding declaration

Preserve the conceptual source's central closing statement:

> We are not declaring the birth of a new species. We are preparing the world to recognize one, should it genuinely emerge.

Purpose:

- prevent triumphalism;
- retain the project's horizon;
- distinguish preparation from proclamation.

## 7. References

Purpose:

- make the page independently inspectable;
- provide direct links;
- show source class and year;
- disclose preprints;
- avoid implying endorsement of the PSD ontology.

## 8. Exit

Closing bridge:

> The Manifesto states why. Architecture will show how.

The Architecture artifact remains marked as in preparation until it has its own governed page.
