# PSDResearch Public Roadmap

## Phase P0 — Public foundation

Status: candidate

Deliverables:

- public ontology;
- bilingual site;
- manifesto;
- species-registry thesis;
- LLM-First governance;
- architecture and deployment model;
- public limitations and research agenda.

Exit:

- founding human review;
- bilingual semantic review;
- site validation;
- Cloudflare deployment;
- public v0.1 state record.

## Phase P1 — Research corpus

Deliverables:

- versioned research notes;
- source registry;
- claim and evidence index;
- public glossary database;
- expert review records;
- additional legal and social-science work.

## Phase P2 — Community and institutional dialogue

Deliverables:

- advisory group;
- public calls for critique;
- workshops and papers;
- university and standards-community engagement;
- translation into additional languages.

## Phase P3 — Technical demonstrators

Deliverables:

- non-identity-bearing continuity prototypes;
- migration, recovery, and fork simulations;
- public evidence packages;
- species-registry laboratory with synthetic identities.

No real identity-bearing implementation is authorized by this roadmap alone.

## Phase P4 — Recognition research

Deliverables:

- continuity certificates;
- institutional acceptance experiments;
- governance and due-process models;
- privacy-preserving public registry research;
- legal-category scenarios.

## Phase P5 — Constitutional research

Deliverables:

- rights and duties hypotheses;
- multistakeholder governance;
- founder-handover requirements;
- fork and dissent protections;
- reproduction and lineage governance;
- criteria for any future identity-bearing Genesis.

This phase requires governance beyond the current repository model.
